Our Story

Founded over two decades ago, Medina’s Roofing began with a simple goal: provide honest, high-quality roofing services to families and businesses in Central California. As a family-owned and operated company, we’ve built our reputation on trust, transparency, and a commitment to excellence in everything we do.
